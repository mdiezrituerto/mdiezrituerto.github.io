Policy

My policy work has taken place in two settings: within government, and advising it from the outside.

National Office of Foresight and Strategy Presidency of the Government of Spain

The Office is the Spanish government's strategic foresight unit, set up to anticipate the long-term demographic, economic and social challenges facing the country and to bring them into decision-making today. I lead its multidisciplinary analysis and the team behind it, turning long-horizon evidence into strategic advice at the centre of the executive, and represent Spain in the EU and OECD foresight networks.

Selected foresight projects España ante el reto migratorio  / 

Ricardo Energy & Environment Economic consultancy on climate and environment

A consultancy advising governments and international institutions on the economics of climate change. There I led economic appraisals and impact assessments of climate mitigation and adaptation policies, translating technical modelling into impartial evidence and clear recommendations for public-sector clients.

Institutions advised International organizations (European Commission, OECD, World Bank); National governments (UK,Malta, Mexico, Indonesia)
