I work at the point where scientific evidence meets policy: using research to inform better decisions, and using the questions that arise in practice to push the scientific frontier

My research examines how institutions, and incentives shape people’s choices, and how these translate into unequal access to opportunitiesg. Alongside it, I work on long-term policy questions at the National Office of Foresight & Strategy, in the Presidency of the Government of Spain.

My work runs in both directions: from research to policy, and from real-world policy challenges back to new research questions.

Research

Labour economics/Gender/Behavioural economics

I study how people make important educational and career decisions, and how competition, incentives, and institutions shape their access to opportunities.
