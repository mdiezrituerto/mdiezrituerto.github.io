Research

Why do people make different choices in comparable circumstances — and how do those choices turn into unequal access to opportunities?

  • Behavioural economics
  • Labour economics

I hold a PhD in Economics from the Universidad del País Vasco, supervised by Nagore Iriberri and Pedro Rey-Biel. Before my doctorate, I trained as an economist at Tilburg University and Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.
